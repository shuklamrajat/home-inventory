## Full description (4000 max)

If you had to claim on your home insurance tomorrow, could you list what you
own and prove what it cost?

ROOM BY ROOM
• Add an item with a photo in seconds
• Rooms ready to go, add your own
• Brand, serial number, purchase date, price paid and today's value
• Search by name, brand or serial

RECEIPTS AND WARRANTIES
• Keep the receipt photo with the item
• Enter the warranty length and the end date is worked out for you
• See which warranties end soon, which are active and which have expired

CLAIM-READY REPORT
• One PDF with every item, its value and its photos and receipts, room by room
• A CSV for spreadsheets
• Total value of everything you own, always up to date

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account, no cloud: photos and details stay on your phone
• Delete an item and its photos are deleted too

HOME INVENTORY PRO
• Unlimited items (the free version holds 25)
