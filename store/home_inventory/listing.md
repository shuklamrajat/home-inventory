## Full description

```text
If you had to claim on your home insurance tomorrow, could you list everything you own and prove what it cost? Housetally is a home inventory app that makes it easy: snap a photo, add the price, and every item lands in its room with its receipt and warranty, ready for a claim.

No account, no sign-up, and your photos and details stay on your phone.

✨ HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a room: Living room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Office and Garage are ready, or add your own
2. Add an item with a photo, what you paid and, if you like, its receipt and warranty
3. Watch your home's total value add up, and export a claim-ready report whenever you need it

🏠 ROOM BY ROOM
• Add an item with photos from your camera or gallery in seconds
• Brand, category, serial number, purchase date, price paid and today's value
• Each room shows how many items it holds and what they're worth
• See your home's total value, always up to date
• A colourful ring shows which rooms hold the most value
• Search everything by name, brand, category or serial number

🧾 RECEIPTS AND WARRANTIES
• Keep the receipt photo with the item, so proof of purchase is never lost
• Enter the warranty length in months and the end date is worked out for you, even across month ends and leap years
• The Warranties tab lists what's ending soon first, with a countdown in days
• See at a glance which warranties are active and which have expired
• A bar on each warranty shows how much of it has been used

📄 CLAIM-READY REPORT (PRO)
• One PDF with every item, room by room, with its value, photos and receipts
• A summary with item count, total value and number of photos and receipts
• A CSV file for Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers
• Share it straight to your insurer, your email or your cloud drive

🏡 MEET HOUSETALLY
Housetally is a little house that keeps count, so you don't have to. It's designed to feel good to use: smooth animations, rooms that fill up as you add things, and a warm dark mode for late-night counting. Prefer less movement? Housetally follows your phone's reduce-motion setting.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account and no sign-up
• One small banner ad; nothing you enter is ever used for ads
• Your photos and details are stored on your phone and never uploaded by us
• Delete an item and its photos and receipts are deleted from your phone too
• Exports only go where you choose to send them

💡 WHY KEEP A HOME INVENTORY?
After a fire, flood or burglary, insurers usually ask for a list of what was lost and what it was worth. Most people can't remember half of what they own, and every item left off the list is money left unclaimed. A home inventory with photos and receipts makes a claim faster and fairer, and it's just as useful for moving house, renting a flat, or checking whether something is still under warranty.

❓ GOOD TO KNOW
• Works offline: add items anywhere, even in the basement with no signal
• Today's value is optional; if you leave it blank, the price you paid is used
• Pick your currency in Settings
• Light or dark theme, or follow your phone

WHO IT'S FOR
Homeowners and renters getting their insurance in order, families keeping track of electronics, appliances, furniture and jewellery, people moving house, landlords listing what's in a furnished let, and anyone who wants their warranties in one place.

FREE AND PRO
Housetally is free for up to 25 items, with rooms, photos, receipts and warranties. Housetally Pro adds unlimited items and the insurance PDF and CSV export: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once for life. Prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy.

Housetally helps you keep records for insurance. It doesn't value items or guarantee a claim, so check what your insurer needs.

Questions or ideas? We read every message, and the best ideas end up in the app.
```
